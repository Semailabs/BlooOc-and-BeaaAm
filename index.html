Why Block & Beam

Built by people who once moved to a new city with two suitcases

Less like a property portal, more like a friend who already knows the neighbourhood.

Verified hosts

Every host completes identity verification before their room ever gets listed.

Student friendly

Built with exchange programmes and short academic terms in mind, not year-long corporate leases.

Transparent pricing

The number you see is the number you pay. No surprise admin fees at checkout.

Secure messaging

Talk with your host, share documents, and confirm details before you ever hand over a deposit.

Flexible stays

Book for a few weeks or a full academic year — the terms adjust to your calendar, not ours.

Local community

Every listing comes with the nearest cafés, groceries and transport links, mapped out for you.
